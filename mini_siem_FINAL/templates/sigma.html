Sigma Rules
Sigma is the open, vendor-neutral YAML detection-rule format used across the SIEM industry (Splunk, Elastic, Sentinel, Wazuh, Chronicle all support importing Sigma rules). Rules here run against every incoming log alongside the built-in detection rules. This is a simplified interpreter — it supports single/multi-selection AND/OR conditions with exact and wildcard (*text*) field matching, which covers the large majority of real Sigma rules.

Paste a Sigma-format YAML rule. Required fields: title, detection.condition.
